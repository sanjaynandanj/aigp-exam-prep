The method

Reading is not preparation. This system is built around questions, spaced repetition, and a fixed exam date.

01Book the exam first

Step 0 of every track is scheduling the real exam. A fixed date concentrates effort; "when I feel ready" never arrives.

02Blueprint-weighted hours

Domains II–IV carry ~70 of 85 scored questions. The plan allocates hours accordingly — the EU AI Act alone gets two full passes.

03Questions early and often

A 20-question diagnostic before any studying, a gated 20-question quiz after every domain, and an 85-question timed mock in the final week.

04The question journal

Every miss gets logged with why you missed it, then re-tested at +2 and +7 days. The journal, not the notes, is the real study asset.

05Memory hooks per lesson

Each lesson ends with mnemonics, contrast tables, and "the 5 facts to retain" — the compression layer for final-week review.

06Background-aware shortcuts

CIPP/E holders skim the GDPR lessons; CIPM holders skim program-building; everyone else takes the longer track. No wasted hours.
