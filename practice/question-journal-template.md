# Question Journal — Template

The question journal is the highest-leverage tool in this curriculum: every question you miss (or guess luckily on) becomes a scheduled, personal micro-lesson. Misses that never get journaled get missed again on exam day.

## How to use it

1. **Log immediately.** Within 24 hours of any quiz or mock, add one row per miss *and* per lucky guess.
2. **Diagnose honestly.** The "why I missed it" column has exactly three allowed values:
   - **knowledge** — you didn't know the fact/rule;
   - **misread** — you knew it but misparsed the stem (missed a NOT/EXCEPT/MOST/FIRST qualifier, skimmed the scenario);
   - **trap** — you fell for a designed confusion (GDPR vs AI Act, provider vs deployer, MAP vs MEASURE, disparate treatment vs impact, HITL vs HOTL...).
3. **Write the rule as one line you'd bet on.** Not "review Article 22" but "human involvement must be *meaningful* (authority + competence + influence) to escape Art. 22."
4. **Re-test at +2 days:** cover the answer, re-answer from the question reference only. Record ✔/✘.
5. **Re-test at +7 days:** same. Two ✘ = make a flashcard and add the rule to your final-48-hours sheet.
6. **Weekly:** scan the "why" column. Lots of *misread* → slow down and underline qualifiers. Lots of *trap* → build/extend your contrast tables. Lots of *knowledge* in one domain → reopen that phase.

**Column notes**

- **Source** — `baseline`, `quiz-domain-1..4`, `mock-exam`, or any external question set.
- **Q#** — enough to find the question again without copying it (copying wastes time and spoils the +2d re-test).
- **Domain** — I–IV; this is what lets you see per-domain weakness trends at a glance.
- **Retest results** — ✔ answered correctly cold; ✘ missed again. An empty cell means the re-test is overdue: do it.
