# Rest of World: UK, Canada, China, Singapore, Brazil, and the Asia-Pacific

**Phase 4 · Lesson 11 · ~1h · BoK Domain II**

## EXAM FOCUS

The exam samples the world map lightly but predictably: one question comparing **regulatory postures** (who has a comprehensive law vs sector guidance vs vertical rules), and one or two on signature national features (UK's pro-innovation stance, China's vertical measures + filings, Singapore's AI Verify, Canada's AIDA failure). Learn each jurisdiction as a compact table: *approach · key instruments · enforcement · one distinctive fact*. Several are in flux — flag and verify.

## CORE

### United Kingdom — pro-innovation, regulator-led

| Attribute | Content |
|---|---|
| Approach | **No single AI act.** The 2023 white paper "A pro-innovation approach to AI regulation": five **cross-sectoral principles** applied by **existing regulators** within their remits, initially on a **non-statutory** basis |
| The five principles | Safety, security & robustness · appropriate transparency & explainability · fairness · accountability & governance · contestability & redress |
| Machinery | Sector regulators (ICO, FCA, CMA, Ofcom, MHRA…) publish AI strategies; **DSIT** central function coordinates; **AI Safety Institute → renamed AI Security Institute (2025)** for frontier-model evaluation; DRCF cooperation forum |
| Legislative outlook | Government has signalled possible **targeted legislation on the most powerful frontier models**; a private members' AI bill circulated without government backing — **status as of mid-2026: verify** |
| Adjacent hard law | UK GDPR (incl. Art. 22 analog — reformed by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 to relax some ADM rules), Equality Act 2010, Online Safety Act; hosted the 2023 **Bletchley Summit** |
| Distinctive fact | Deliberate contrast with the EU: *principles + regulators, not statute + CE marking* |

### Canada — AIDA died; provinces and existing law fill the gap

| Attribute | Content |
|---|---|
| The AIDA story | The **Artificial Intelligence and Data Act** (part of Bill C-27, introduced 2022) would have regulated "high-impact" AI federally — but **C-27 died on the Order Paper when Parliament was prorogued in January 2025**. AIDA is **not law**; any successor framework remains to be introduced — **status as of mid-2026: verify** |
| What applies instead | **PIPEDA** (federal private-sector privacy) + provincial laws; **Quebec's Law 25**: transparency + pre-decision notice for **fully automated decisions** and explanation/review rights — currently Canada's most concrete ADM rule; human-rights codes; the **Directive on Automated Decision-Making** (binding on **federal government** systems: Algorithmic Impact Assessment tiers, notice, human-in-the-loop by impact level) |
| Voluntary layer | Voluntary **Code of Conduct on Advanced Generative AI** (2023); a federal AI strategy/ministerial focus on adoption post-2025 |
| Distinctive fact | Cautionary tale the exam likes: an early comprehensive bill that **failed procedurally** — "first-mover bills don't always land" |

### China — state-led, vertical, filing-based

| Attribute | Content |
|---|---|
| Approach | No single AI act (a unified law long rumored — verify); instead **vertical measures per technology**, enforced primarily by the **CAC**, with security assessments and **algorithm filings** to a public registry |
| The big three measures | **Algorithmic Recommendation Provisions (2022)**: filing, opt-out of personalization, no "big-data price discrimination," worker-scheduling fairness · **Deep Synthesis Provisions (2023)**: consent + **conspicuous labeling of synthetic media** (deepfakes) · **Interim Measures for Generative AI (2023)**: security assessment + filing before public-facing launch, training-data legality, content aligned with **core socialist values**, labeling, provider responsibility for outputs |
| 2025 addition | **AI-generated content labeling measures** (explicit + implicit/metadata labels) effective Sept 2025 |
| Foundations | PIPL (GDPR-analog with state carve-outs), Data Security Law, Cybersecurity Law; data-localization/cross-border rules |
| Distinctive facts | **Pre-launch state review** of public LLMs; content control as a first-class regulatory goal; ~global first-mover on binding GenAI rules |

### Singapore — voluntary frameworks + testing tools

| Attribute | Content |
|---|---|
| Approach | **No binding horizontal AI law** — deliberate soft-governance strategy by IMDA/PDPC |
| Instruments | **Model AI Governance Framework** (2019/2020: internal governance, human oversight levels, ops management, stakeholder communication) · **Model AI Governance Framework for Generative AI (2024)** — nine dimensions (accountability, data, trusted development, incident reporting, testing/assurance, security, content provenance, safety R&D, public good) |
| Signature tool | **AI Verify** (2022): the world's first **AI governance testing framework + software toolkit** — process checks + technical tests against 11 principles; open-sourced via the **AI Verify Foundation**; complemented by **Project Moonshot** (LLM red-teaming/benchmarking toolkit, 2024) |
| Distinctive fact | Exports *assurance tooling* rather than law — "test, don't legislate (yet)"; sectoral rules (MAS FEAT principles for finance) fill gaps |

### Brazil — the EU-style bill in progress

| Attribute | Content |
|---|---|
| Approach | **PL 2338/2023** — comprehensive, **risk-based** AI bill explicitly inspired by the EU AI Act: excessive-risk (prohibited) and high-risk tiers, provider/deployer ("agent") duties, algorithmic impact assessments, civil-liability provisions, rights of affected persons, a governance system coordinated by the data-protection authority **ANPD** (SIA system) |
| Status | **Approved by the Senate December 2024; under consideration in the Chamber of Deputies** thereafter — **not yet law; status as of mid-2026: verify** |
| Applicable today | **LGPD** (GDPR-analog, incl. Art. 20 right to **review of automated decisions**), consumer code, ANPD enforcement (it has acted on AI training of personal data by major platforms) |
| Distinctive fact | Likely the clearest **Brussels-effect** case study in the Americas if enacted |

### Japan, South Korea, India — mention level

| Country | Posture in one line |
|---|---|
| **Japan** | Innovation-first "agile governance": the **AI Promotion Act (2025)** — a *basic law* promoting AI development with duties of effort, **no penalties**; soft guidance (METI/AI Guidelines for Business); relies on existing laws (APPI privacy, broad TDM copyright exception); G7 Hiroshima champion |
| **South Korea** | **AI Framework/Basic Act (enacted Dec 2024/Jan 2025, effective Jan 2026)** — second comprehensive national AI law after the EU: risk-based duties for "high-impact" AI + GenAI transparency/labeling, domestic-representative requirement, modest fines; promotion + regulation in one statute — verify implementing decrees |
| **India** | **No AI-specific law**; DPDP Act 2023 (privacy) + IT rules; MeitY advisories on deepfakes/model deployment (2024, revised after backlash); pro-innovation "AI for All"/IndiaAI Mission posture; deepfake-labeling amendments to IT rules proposed 2025 — verify |

## CASES & EXAMPLES

**Scenario 1 — Posture matching.** A stem describes a country that "issued cross-sectoral principles for existing regulators to apply, declining to legislate a single AI statute." *Analysis:* The **UK** — distinguish from Singapore (voluntary frameworks + testing tools, IMDA-led) by the regulator-application mechanism and the white-paper language.

**Scenario 2 — Launching an LLM in China.** A US firm wants its chatbot available to the Chinese public. *Analysis:* Generative AI Interim Measures: **security assessment + algorithm filing before launch**, training-data legality, output moderation aligned with content rules, synthetic-content labeling (explicit and embedded, per the 2025 labeling measures), and PIPL/data-export compliance. Contrast: none of this pre-launch state review exists in the US/UK/Singapore.

**Scenario 3 — Advising on Canada.** In 2026 a client asks what "AIDA compliance" requires. *Analysis:* Trick question — AIDA never became law (died with prorogation, Jan 2025). Actual obligations: PIPEDA/provincial privacy, Quebec Law 25's automated-decision transparency/review rights, human-rights statutes, and (if selling to the federal government) the ADM Directive's Algorithmic Impact Assessment. Verify whether a successor bill has appeared.

**Scenario 4 — The assurance pitch.** A Singapore fintech wants to demonstrate trustworthy AI to regulators and clients without waiting for legislation. *Analysis:* Run **AI Verify** process checks + technical tests (and Project Moonshot red-teaming for its LLM features), aligned to the Model AI Governance Frameworks and MAS FEAT principles — Singapore's whole regulatory theory in one engagement.

## MEMORY HOOKS

- **Five-word national slogans:** UK — *principles, not statutes* · Canada — *the bill that died* · China — *file first, launch later* · Singapore — *test, don't legislate* · Brazil — *Brussels effect, Senate approved* · Japan — *promote, don't punish* · Korea — *second comprehensive law* · India — *advisories only, for now.*
- **Comprehensive-law club (enacted):** EU + **South Korea** (+ Colorado at state level). Everyone else: pending, partial, or purposefully without.
- **China's vertical trio:** *Recommend (2022) → Synthesize (2023) → Generate (2023)* — each with filing + labeling DNA.
- **Flux flags:** Canada successor bill, Brazil PL 2338, UK frontier legislation, India IT-rule amendments — all "verify current status (mid-2026)."

**The 5 facts to retain:**
1. The UK governs AI through five cross-sectoral principles applied by existing regulators — no single act; frontier-model legislation only signalled.
2. Canada's AIDA (Bill C-27) died with the January 2025 prorogation; Quebec Law 25 and the federal ADM Directive are the operative Canadian ADM rules.
3. China regulates vertically (recommendation algorithms, deep synthesis, generative AI) with CAC filings, pre-launch security assessments, and mandatory synthetic-content labeling — state-led content control included.
4. Singapore leads with voluntary frameworks and **AI Verify**, the first governance testing toolkit; Brazil's EU-inspired PL 2338 passed the Senate (Dec 2024) but wasn't yet law.
5. South Korea enacted the second comprehensive national AI law (AI Basic Act, effective Jan 2026); Japan chose a penalty-free promotion act; India relies on privacy law and advisories.

## SELF-CHECK

1. **Q:** Name the UK's five AI principles and who applies them.
   **A:** Safety/security/robustness; appropriate transparency & explainability; fairness; accountability & governance; contestability & redress — applied by existing sector regulators (ICO, FCA, Ofcom, CMA…) on a non-statutory basis, coordinated centrally.

2. **Q:** What happened to Canada's AIDA and what governs Canadian automated decisions meanwhile?
   **A:** AIDA (within Bill C-27) died when Parliament was prorogued in January 2025 without passage. Meanwhile: PIPEDA and provincial privacy laws, Quebec Law 25's automated-decision notice/explanation/review rights, and the federal Directive on ADM (with Algorithmic Impact Assessments) for government systems. Verify successor developments.

3. **Q:** List China's three core vertical AI measures and the common procedural thread.
   **A:** Algorithmic Recommendation Provisions (2022), Deep Synthesis Provisions (2023), Interim Measures for Generative AI (2023) — all feature CAC algorithm filing/security assessment and labeling/content obligations before or during public operation.

4. **Q:** What is AI Verify?
   **A:** Singapore's open-source AI governance **testing framework and software toolkit** (IMDA, 2022) combining technical tests and process checks against internationally aligned principles — voluntary assurance instead of regulation, extended to LLM red-teaming via Project Moonshot.

5. **Q:** Which non-EU jurisdictions had *enacted* comprehensive/risk-based AI statutes, and which had prominent pending bills (status mid-2026 — verify)?
   **A:** Enacted: South Korea's AI Basic Act (framework, effective Jan 2026); Japan's AI Promotion Act exists but is promotion-only. Pending: Brazil's PL 2338 (Senate-approved), possible Canadian successor to AIDA, UK frontier-model bill — all to be verified.
