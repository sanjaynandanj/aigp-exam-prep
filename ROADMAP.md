# ROADMAP — three time-bound tracks

Same 72 lessons, three paces. **Step 0 of every track: book the real exam.** A fixed date beats motivation every week of the plan.

| Track | Duration | Weekly load | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sprint** | 4 weeks | ~20 h | Deadline-driven; CIPP/E-CIPM holders; full-time study window |
| **Default** | 8 weeks | ~10 h | Working professionals; first IAPP certification |
| **Steady** | 12 weeks | ~7 h | Heavy quarters; no prior privacy or AI exposure |

Universal rules:

- **Diagnostic before study.** Take `practice/baseline-diagnostic.md` cold on Day 1. It sets your per-domain effort.
- **The question journal is non-negotiable.** Every missed question gets a row (`practice/question-journal-template.md`) and a re-test at +2 days and +7 days.
- **One-page summaries.** Close each phase by writing its one-pager from memory; check against the lessons' MEMORY HOOKS.
- **Two passes on the EU AI Act.** Nothing else on the exam rewards precision as much.
- **Quiz gate:** score 14+/20 on a domain quiz before advancing; below 14, re-study the flagged lessons and retake within 3 days.

## Default track — 8 weeks (recommended)

### Week 1 — Phase 0 + Phase 1 (~10h)
- **Day 1:** P0-01, P0-02 (exam logistics, BoK map). **Book the exam for ~Week 9.**
- **Day 2:** P0-03, P0-04 + take the 20-Q baseline diagnostic. Log misses.
- **Day 3–5:** P1 lessons 01–06 (definitions, ML types, deep learning, genAI, stack).
- **Weekend (3h):** P1 lessons 07–10 incl. the 40-term vocabulary drill. Write the Phase 1 one-pager.

### Week 2 — Phase 2 (~10h)
- **Day 1–5 (1.5h/day):** P2 lessons 01–07 (harms, trustworthy-AI attributes, fairness, XAI, oversight, privacy, ethics frameworks).
- **Weekend:** P2 lessons 08–10 (program, roles, stakeholders). One-pager. +2/+7 journal re-tests.

### Week 3 — Phase 3, first half (~10h)
- **Day 1–2:** P3-01, P3-02 (GDPR × AI, Article 22 — slow down here; it's an exam favorite).
- **Day 3:** P3-03 (DPIAs, enforcement).
- **Day 4–5:** P3-04, P3-05 (US federal, US state laws).
- **Weekend:** **Domain I quiz** (covers Phases 1–2). Gate: 14+/20. Journal misses.

### Week 4 — Phase 3 finish + Phase 4 start (~10h)
- **Day 1–3:** P3-06 → P3-10 (IP, discrimination, liability, sectoral, cross-border).
- **Day 4–5:** P4-01, P4-02 (AI Act structure & actors; prohibited practices — memorize the Article 5 list cold).
- **Weekend:** P4-03, P4-04 (high-risk classification + obligations). Classification flowchart drill: run 10 hypothetical systems through it.

### Week 5 — Phase 4 finish (~10h)
- **Day 1–2:** P4-05, P4-06, P4-07 (GPAI, conformity/CE, enforcement & timeline).
- **Day 3:** P4-08 (NIST AI RMF — build your own GOVERN/MAP/MEASURE/MANAGE table from memory).
- **Day 4:** P4-09, P4-10 (ISO 42001/23894, soft law).
- **Day 5:** P4-11 (rest of world).
- **Weekend:** P4-12 crosswalk drill. **Domain II quiz.** Gate: 14+/20.

### Week 6 — Phase 5 (~10h)
- **Day 1–5 (1.5h/day):** P5 lessons 01–07 (lifecycle, intake, data governance, training, testing, impact assessments, documentation).
- **Weekend:** P5-08 → P5-10 (security, release gates, provider vs deployer). One-pager. **Domain III quiz.**

### Week 7 — Phase 6 (~10h)
- **Day 1–5:** P6 lessons 01–07 (readiness, vendor risk, procurement, monitoring, oversight, transparency, incidents).
- **Weekend:** P6-08 → P6-10 (audits, decommissioning, org change). One-pager. **Domain IV quiz.**

### Week 8 — Phase 7: exam week (~12h)
- **Day 1:** P7-01 question anatomy + review all four one-pagers.
- **Day 2:** **Full 85-Q mock, timed (165 min), exam conditions.** Score it; run the retro protocol.
- **Day 3:** Rework every mock miss; re-read the lessons behind your two weakest domains.
- **Day 4:** EU AI Act second pass: prohibited list, Annex III areas, GPAI duties, penalties, timeline — from memory, then verify.
- **Day 5:** Journal sweep: re-test every question still marked open. Light review only.
- **Day 6:** Rest or 30-minute skim of MEMORY HOOKS. No new material.
- **Exam day:** ~1.9 min/question; flag and return; answer everything (no penalty).

## Sprint track — 4 weeks

Compression rule: two Default weeks per week; quizzes keep their gates.

| Week | Content | Checkpoints |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Phases 0–2 | Baseline Day 1 · Domain I quiz weekend |
| 2 | Phases 3–4 | Domain II quiz weekend · AI Act flowchart drill |
| 3 | Phases 5–6 | Domain III + IV quizzes |
| 4 | Phase 7 | Mock Day 2 · AI Act second pass · journal sweep · exam |

CIPP/E holders: skim P3-01→03 and reinvest in Phase 4. CIPM holders: skim P2-08→10.

## Steady track — 12 weeks

Expansion rule: Default weeks 1–8 stretch to 1.5×. Extra time goes to: a second reading of Phase 4 in Week 9, a **second full mock** in Week 11 (re-take the same 85 questions — target 75+), and a weekly 30-minute flashcard session built from GLOSSARY.md high-yield terms.

| Weeks | Content |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Phases 0–1 |
| 3–4 | Phase 2 + Domain I quiz |
| 5–6 | Phase 3 |
| 7–8 | Phase 4 + Domain II quiz |
| 9 | Phase 4 second pass + crosswalk |
| 10 | Phase 5 + Domain III quiz |
| 11 | Phase 6 + Domain IV quiz + mock #1 |
| 12 | Phase 7: mock #2, retros, journal sweep, exam |

## Weekly self-audit (all tracks)

Every weekend, answer in `journal.md`:

1. Hours actually spent vs planned? (>20% short two weeks running → switch to the next-longer track and move the exam once, early — not the week before.)
2. Latest quiz score and the two weakest topics?
3. Are all journal rows current on their +2/+7 re-tests?
4. Can you still write the Article 5 prohibited list and the four NIST RMF functions from memory?
